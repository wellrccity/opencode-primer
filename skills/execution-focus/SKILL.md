# Execution focus

1. Freeze the goal as "done when ___".
2. Keep at most five steps, each one small and verifiable.
3. Do one step at a time; record blockers without opening parallel fronts.
4. When interrupted, record `State / Literal next step / Files touched` in `HANDOFF.md`.
5. Prefer verifiable progress over unrequested refactors; record technical debt without hiding it.

Reply per turn: a short checklist at the top when work is active; at the end, a single next step.
